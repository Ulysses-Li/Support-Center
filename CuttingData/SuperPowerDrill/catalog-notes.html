How is catalog data used?

  • The 81 standard drill bodies are filtered by nominal diameter, catalog drilling depth T and Ø32 mm rear shank. Marketing depth ratios do not generate part numbers.
  • All 20 cutting rows are retained. Physical center and periphery inserts and grades are checked separately; the user explicitly selects the 7D class.
  • The table gives range endpoints. Site starting values are adjustable arithmetic midpoints, not catalog optimums. Feed f is in mm/rev and F=S×f, without multiplying by insert count.
  • Candidates are shown only for materials with an applicable pairing in this series' cutting table. Non-ferrous metals are not recommended. Heat-resistant alloys and other unlisted materials receive no recommendation, and manual values cannot bypass the restriction. Supported materials with incomplete settings may use a labeled manual calculation; prohibited applications or insufficient coolant stop calculation.
  • The line drawing is not to scale; dimensions update with the part number. L1 is the front dimension, rear shank length is separately listed as 58 mm, and T is used to screen required hole depth.
  • Upper and lower diameter deviations record the customer's requirements. They are not a catalog guarantee and do not widen standard tool diameters.

Metric S and F use general unit-conversion mathematics, without attributing another chapter's formulas to this PDF. The p134 Kc table and force formulas remain reference data until definitions are complete.

Manufacturer questions

P1 affects the relevant automatic recommendation or ordering. P2 can be addressed in stages. Missing information is not replaced with another material or product's data.

Q01 · P1 · p135 ↗

Basis for selecting the 7D cutting row

Does T=Length/Dia. on p135 use the required hole depth or the selected body's listed T? Which row applies at exactly 7D? Please provide the formal selection rule.

The user currently selects T<7D or T>7D; the class is not inferred from body length.

Q02 · P1 · p130 ↗、p133 ↗

Usable hole depth, tip datum and bottom profile

Please provide the axial difference between center tip and periphery edge, full cylindrical-hole depth, blind-hole bottom profile and through-hole breakthrough allowance, and clarify listed T versus programmed tool-tip Z.

Required hole depth is used to screen catalog T; it does not automatically generate programmed Z.

Q03 · P1 · p130 ↗、p131 ↗、p132 ↗、p135 ↗

Full NC40 periphery order codes and body compatibility

p132 lists N9GX06020431 / N9GX09030831, while p135 uses family headings without 31. Confirm compatible standard bodies, screws and full ordering format, plus complete CD6 / CD8 order codes for each grade.

Automatic pairing currently uses listed physical NC2032 periphery inserts. Center grade is selected as NC2032 or NC40 by material row, without inventing suffixes.

Q04 · P1 · p131 ↗、p132 ↗

Periphery screw discrepancy for Ø30–34

The p131 body table lists NS-22055; the p132 insert table lists NS-22062 for the same family. Confirm the correct screw, length and revision for each body and insert.

Both source values and the on-screen note are retained; neither is overwritten globally.

Q05 · P2 · p130 ↗、p131 ↗

Two dimensional / conversion discrepancies

p130 prints 1.630 inches for Ø27; p131 lists L=346 mm for 00-99307-39300. Confirm possible misprints and provide corrections.

Metric nominal diameters are filtered as listed; L=346 mm is retained for that part.

Q06 · P2 · p130 ↗、p135 ↗

Minimum clamping and coolant requirements

Provide minimum engagement for the Ø32 rear shank, allowed overhang, flow recommendations by diameter and depth, and pressure measurement location. The catalog only specifies at least 10 bar and high flow; 58 mm is the rear-shank dimension.

Calculation stops with no internal coolant or pressure below 10 bar. Unconfirmed coolant or pressure produces a condition note.

Q07 · P2 · p132 ↗、p134 ↗、p135 ↗

Material boundaries and applicability

How are C=0.3% and HRC=50 classified? This series is not recommended for non-ferrous metals, and no applicable pairing is listed for heat-resistant materials. If the manufacturer formally changes applicability, provide matching physical inserts and cutting data. The p134 Kc table does not replace this tool's speed and feed data.

Non-ferrous, heat-resistant and other unlisted materials receive neither recommendations nor manual calculations. Reassess only after a formal applicability change.

Q08 · P2 · p133 ↗、p135 ↗

Entry / breakthrough rules and adjustment scope

Can the horizontal-spindle +20% and chip-based ±25% adjustments be combined? Provide staged entry and breakthrough distances, feed ratios and hole restrictions. The catalog gives no universal rule to halve feed for the first 3 mm.

Factors are not automatically combined. Stack drilling and angled surfaces stop automatic calculation as specified on p135.

Q09 · P2 · p134 ↗

Definitions for thrust, torque and Kc formulas

Define ap in the p134 formula, confirm the Md constant and units, and provide a metric example and the relationship between power and machine efficiency.

This page currently uses only metric S=1000×Vc/(π×D) and F=S×f. It does not output thrust, torque or power.
