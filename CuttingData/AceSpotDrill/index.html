Compensation & Z reference

XY follows the original contour at its original size. R is the tool-center distance to that contour, toward the free side. Apply this geometry offset only once.

Select G41 or G42 from travel direction and the free side. The D address selects an offset register; it is not the numerical radius shown here.

These are full geometry values, not CAM wear-only offsets. Z0 is the work surface; the selected tool length reference determines the negative Z target. Local straight-edge geometry does not verify corners, hole clearance, holders, or lead-in/out moves.
